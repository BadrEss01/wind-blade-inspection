## Research focus

The project investigated a cooperative inspection system: a UAV transports a ground robot to a wind turbine blade, a deployment mechanism places it on the surface, and the robot moves along the blade to support contact inspection.

My undergraduate research assistant work focused on the ground robot, combining research into adhesion and locomotion with mechanical design and prototyping.

## Main design outcome

The ground-robot study led to a concept using passive suction cups on two belt-driven climbing modules. A guide rail manages attachment and release, while a swivel connects the modules to the payload chassis. The report includes CAD illustrations and a photograph of a 3D-printed guide rail.

The force analysis and proposed test rig outline how adhesion could be evaluated. The report does not provide a measured operating load or demonstrate deployment on an operational wind turbine.
